About

Dr. Tim Riddell

Dr. Tim Riddell is a distinguished physician-scientist with over two decades of clinical and research experience. His work bridges cutting-edge biomedical research and compassionate patient care, with a particular focus on advancing diagnostic and therapeutic approaches in internal medicine.

A frequent speaker at international medical conferences, Dr. Riddell is passionate about translating complex scientific findings into practical clinical insights that improve patient outcomes worldwide.

Research Focus

Areas of Expertise

Dr. Riddell's research spans several critical domains of modern medicine, each aimed at improving patient outcomes and advancing clinical knowledge.

🫀

Cardiovascular Medicine

Investigating novel biomarkers and therapeutic targets in heart failure, with a focus on early intervention strategies and personalised treatment protocols.

🧬

Precision Oncology

Applying genomic profiling and immunotherapy advances to tailor cancer treatment plans, improving response rates while minimising adverse effects.

🧠

Neurological Disorders

Exploring the intersection of inflammation and neurodegeneration, seeking disease-modifying therapies for Alzheimer's and Parkinson's disease.

💉

Clinical Trials Design

Developing robust methodologies for multi-centre randomised controlled trials, ensuring statistical rigour and ethical integrity.

📊

Health Data Analytics

Leveraging large-scale electronic health records and machine learning models to uncover population-level health trends and risk predictors.

🌐

Global Health Equity

Advocating for equitable access to diagnostics and treatment in low-resource settings, in partnership with international health bodies.

Key Findings

Evidence Highlights

38% Reduction in CV Events

RIDDELL-HEART Trial (2024): Novel troponin-guided therapy protocol reduced major adverse cardiac events in high-risk populations vs. standard care.

2× Faster Diagnosis

AI-assisted imaging platform halved diagnostic time for pulmonary embolism in emergency settings across 6 NHS Trusts (2025 multicentre study).

62% Response Rate

Personalised immunotherapy protocol, guided by tumour microenvironment profiling, achieved superior response in NSCLC patients versus pembrolizumab monotherapy.

£4.2M Cost Savings

Predictive analytics model deployed across 3 hospital trusts identified avoidable readmissions, delivering significant cost efficiency over 18 months.
